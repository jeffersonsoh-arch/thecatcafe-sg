About us
Home to 15 rescued cats, coffee & art jamming

The Cat Cafe is home to 15 cats and operates at Bugis with art jamming. We welcome those who love cats and enjoy engaging with people in a unique cafe environment. As Cafe Supervisor, you'll lead daily cafe operations, ensure the wellbeing of our cats, and manage your team to keep things running smoothly for both cats and customers.

The role
Responsibilities
●Lead daily cafe operations to ensure smooth functioning and customer satisfaction
●Coach and train team members to uphold service and cat care standards
●Manage cafe inventory to maintain adequate supplies and reduce waste
●Brief customers on cafe rules to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience
●Prepare and serve food and drinks to customers following hygiene standards
●Engage customers by explaining cat behaviours and proper handling techniques
●Groom cats regularly and monitor their health to maintain wellbeing
●Clean cats' living areas, including condos, beds and litter pans, to ensure hygiene
●Maintain cleanliness and presentation of cat condos and refresh litter pans daily
●Prepare daily meals and water bowls for cats according to dietary needs
●Perform dishwashing and laundry duties to support cafe operations
●Execute any additional duties assigned to support cafe functions
What we're looking for
Skills & qualifications

No specific certifications are required. A diploma in Food & Beverage Management/Services is preferred, along with a genuine love for cats and people.
